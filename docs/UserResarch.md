# Observation 1: A majority of students have not used the spreadsheet to find their class

## Observation Statement: The google spreadsheet is the only place that tells students if their class is being offered at a certain time or not. Because a majority of the students have not used the spreadsheet, it means there is no easy nor universally known method of checking the AEC schedule.

### Supporting Data: The responses to the question: "Have you used the AEC drop-in spreadsheet to find if your class is covered?", had 65% respond with "No" and 35% respond with "Yes"

### Team Action (if any): This gives us clear motivation to continue with the app development.

# Observation 2: A majority of students have gone to the AEC and found the subject they needed was closed

## Observation Statement: Students currently have no reliable way to know whether a subject is open before they travel to the AEC. More than half of respondents have made a trip and found the subject they needed closed, which means they wasted time and did not get the help they came for.

### Supporting Data: The responses to the question: "Have you ever gone to the AEC, but the subject you needed was closed?" (20 responses) had 55% respond with "Yes" (11 students) and 45% respond with "No" (9 students)

### Team Action (if any): This supports prioritizing real time subject status and notifications when a subject opens in the app, so students can check before they go.

# Observation 3: Most desired feature is "Search for tutoring by course code / subject"

## Observation Statement:  The greatest desire from students who use the AEC, is to have an easy method to check which classes are available

### Supporting Data: It is most common response to: "If the AEC offered a mobile-friendly app, what would you most want it to help you accomplish the most?", with a 71.4% response rate. 

### Team Action (if any): Focus attention to making this feature the focal point of the app.

# Observation 4: Notifications displaying wait times / waitlist openings to the user is a feature that is user wanted  

## Observation Statement: Most students want a real-time notification system to alert them if their specific (or related) course is available to be taught in the AEC at a certain time 

### Supporting Data: 28.6% of survey responders state they want auto-notifications when a waitlist spot opens. 61.9% of survey responders state they want "real-time drop in availability"

### Team Action: Automatic notification feature is necessary in order to alert the user of any available spots for tutoring.
